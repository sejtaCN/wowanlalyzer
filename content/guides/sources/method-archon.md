## Archon Priority（原文顺序，逐条）
1. Cast Shadow Word: Pain if its not active and you are talented into Invoked Nightmare
2. Cast and maintain Vampiric Touch, either with Tentacle Slam or Vampiric Touch
3. Cast Halo
4. Cast Voidform
5. Cast Power Infusion
6. Spend insanity on Shadow Word: Madness if it's not active, is about to fall off, or you are about to cap on insanity
7. Cast Tentacle Slam if you are about to cap on charges or Vampiric Touch is not active
8. Cast Mind Blast
9. Fully consume Tentacle Slam charges if adds are not coming soon you need to hold charges for
10. Cast Mind Flay: Insanity if Shadow Word: Madness is active
11. Cast Shadow Word: Death if the target is below 20% health
12. Cast Void Volley
13. Cast Mind Flay. Interrupt this for any other action as it becomes available

## Archon 起手（新 4 件套）
Mind Blast → Tentacle Slam → Shadow Word: Pain（有 Invoked Nightmare）→ Halo → Void Volley → Voidform
→ Power Infusion（同时用主动饰品与爆发药水）→ Shadow Word: Madness → Void Volley → …

## Archon 起手（无新 4 件套）
Tentacle Slam → Shadow Word: Pain（有 Invoked Nightmare）→ Halo → Voidform
→ Power Infusion（同时饰品/药水）→ Shadow Word: Madness → Void Volley → …
